# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Solo quant / retail trader (the project owner) building and testing strategies against
historical + paper-trade data. Context: personal research tool, used at a desk, likely
multiple monitors, keyboard-driven, long sessions scanning symbols and reviewing signals.
Not built for a broad consumer audience.

## Product Purpose
QuantDesk is a strategy research and paper-trading dashboard: backtest strategies, scan
markets, review auto-trade signals and paper P&L. No real-money execution. Success = fast,
accurate reads of market/strategy state with zero ambiguity about what's real vs. simulated.

## Brand Personality
Precise, disciplined, quiet. A terminal, not a marketing page - density and correctness
over decoration. No hype, no persuasion, no upsell tone anywhere in the UI.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboard cliches: gradient hero-metric cards, glassmorphism, soft rounded
  cards-on-cards, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows, numbered section scaffolding.
- Consumer fintech app look (Robinhood-style): playful color, confetti/celebration
  micro-interactions, oversized friendly type. This is a research tool, not a growth app.

## Design Principles
1. Density over whitespace - screens are dashboards, not landing sections; show the data.
2. Terminal-native, not terminal-themed - real monospace rhythm and dark palette, not a
   surface skin over a generic dashboard layout.
3. Every claim is falsifiable - no vague success language; numbers, states, and the
   "research tool, not financial advice, results are hypothetical" disclaimer stay visible.
4. No decorative color - color (up/down/accent) always carries meaning, never mood.
5. Consistency across panels beats novelty per-panel - one visual language across
   dashboard, backtest, paper, journal, symbol pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No stated WCAG level; default to WCAG AA. Dark-only theme (no light mode requirement).
Standard color-blind-safe considerations apply to up/down (green/red) signal colors -
verify redundant cues (icons/direction arrows, not color alone) where P&L sign matters.
