# Promotion criteria - paper to real money

Committed 2026-07-04. These numbers are FIXED at commitment time and are not
moved afterwards - that is the whole point. If the system cannot clear a bar,
the answer is "not yet", never "lower the bar". The live pass/fail table is on
the RECON page (`/reconcile`), computed by `src/core/paper/reconcile.ts`,
which must mirror these numbers verbatim.

Real-money execution is considered ONLY when ALL of the following hold
simultaneously:

1. **Sample size**: >= 60 closed live paper trades.
2. **Duration**: >= 6 months of live unattended operation (measured from the
   first closed trade).
3. **Live Sharpe**: >= 0.7x the blended walk-forward OOS Sharpe of the live
   roster. Blended WF OOS Sharpe = 0.47 (mean of rsi-reversion 0.50,
   bollinger-reversion 0.47, stoch-reversal 0.44 - FEATURE=both evaluation,
   2026-07-03, SYSTEM_AUDIT_AND_ROADMAP.md Round 3). Bar: live Sharpe >= 0.33.
   Live Sharpe is only computed at >= 30 closed trades; below that this
   criterion is pending, not passed.
4. **Drawdown**: live max drawdown <= 1.25x the worst modeled OOS max drawdown
   across the live roster. Worst modeled = 16.33% (bollinger-reversion,
   FEATURE=both). Bar: live max DD <= 20.4%.
5. **Slippage**: realized fill slippage < 2x the 5bps model. Not measurable on
   paper (paper fills are generated BY the cost model) - this criterion
   activates only during a real-money pilot with minimal size, and must hold
   for the pilot's first 20 fills.

Additional standing conditions (not scored, but any one of them vetoes
promotion regardless of the table):

- The walk-forward evidence backing the roster must not be stale: if the
  roster or its parameters changed since the last WF run, re-run
  `eval-walkforward` / `eval-improvements` first.
- No unresolved data-integrity incident (reconcile-providers failing, gap
  detector alarming) in the last 30 days.
- The drawdown circuit breaker (12%) must not have fired in the last 60 days.

If and when all criteria pass: the first real-money phase is a minimal-size
pilot (single-digit percentage of intended capital) whose only purpose is to
measure criterion 5 - not to make money.

Research tool. Not financial advice. All results are hypothetical.
